About College

Govt. College of Engineering and Technology, Jammu is an autonomous government engineering institution in Jammu. The college offers undergraduate programmes in engineering and focuses on quality teaching, hands-on learning and innovation, so that students grow into skilled and responsible professionals.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in technical education.

Our Mission

To nurture young minds through quality education, research and innovation.

Core Values

Integrity, discipline, curiosity and service to society.

Important Information

  • Type: Government Engineering College (Autonomous)
  • Location: Jammu, Jammu & Kashmir
  • Branches: Computer Science, Electronics & Communication, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical
  • Facilities: Library, Seminar Hall, Sports Ground
